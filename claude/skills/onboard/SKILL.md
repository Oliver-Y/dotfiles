Give a 5-minute orientation to an unfamiliar area of the codebase. The user is new to this area and needs to get productive fast.

Use Explore subagents to survey the area broadly. Prioritize breadth over depth.

Structure your onboarding as:

1. **What is this?** — Plain language purpose. What problem does it solve? Who uses it?
2. **Key files** — The 3-5 most important files to understand, with one-line summaries.
3. **Architecture sketch** — How the pieces fit together. ASCII diagram if helpful.
4. **How to build and test** — Exact commands to build, run tests, and run the thing locally.
5. **Common tasks** — "If you need to X, look at Y." Cover the 3 most likely reasons someone would be here.
6. **Gotchas** — Things that will trip you up. Non-obvious conventions, surprising behavior, known rough edges.
7. **Related areas** — What other modules/directories are closely connected and why.

Keep it practical. This is a cheat sheet, not documentation.
